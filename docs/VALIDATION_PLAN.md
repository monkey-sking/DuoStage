# Pre-hardware validation plan

The launch window is short, so validate the **content loop** before Duo hardware is available. Do not wait for hardware to discover that the prompts are boring.

## What can be tested now

Use the simulator/mock hinge build or a staged two-phone setup. The test is not meant to prove hardware behavior. It tests whether people understand and enjoy:

**Secret → Commit → Reaction → Reveal → Share**

## First 10 sessions

Recruit pairs across at least three contexts: couple, friends, family/creator. Give minimal explanation and observe rather than coach.

Record:
- time from launch to recording start
- whether both participants understand what must stay secret
- whether a visible/verbal commitment happens before reveal
- whether reveal creates a reaction worth keeping
- whether they ask to play another prompt
- whether they want the resulting clip
- whether they invoke/share the exported clip when offered

## Early thresholds

These are kill/iterate thresholds, not long-term KPI targets:
- ≥ 8/10 sessions complete without facilitator rescue
- ≥ 6/10 produce a reaction both participants consider worth keeping
- ≥ 5/10 immediately choose another prompt
- ≥ 4/10 would genuinely post/send the clip

If completion is high but posting intent is low, improve the exported format before adding features. If repeat play is low, improve prompts before camera engineering.

## Prompt quality rubric

Keep prompts that reliably create at least one of:
- disagreement
- recognition
- embarrassment
- affection
- status reversal
- visual contrast
- surprise

Remove trivia that merely produces a correct answer.
