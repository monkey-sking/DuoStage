## Name
DuoStage

## Subtitle
Two sides. One reveal.

## Promotional text
Turn iPhone Duo into a two-sided social stage. Give each person a secret prompt, capture both reactions, then unfold the phone to reveal the moment.

## Description
DuoStage is a social camera built around one simple gesture: unfold to reveal.

Place a partially folded iPhone Duo between two people. Each side gets a different secret prompt. Start recording, commit to your answers or reactions, then open the phone. The physical unfold drives the reveal while DuoStage turns the moment into a vertical reaction video ready to share.

Built for couples, friends, family, creators and spontaneous street challenges.

Launch version focuses on one fast loop:
- two secret sides
- synchronized reactions
- physical unfold reveal
- one-tap vertical export

No feed. No follower graph. No editing timeline. The point is the moment between two people.

## Review notes draft
DuoStage uses the camera and microphone only while the user explicitly starts a reaction recording. The app uses iPhone Duo-specific hinge/display capabilities to present separate prompts and drive the reveal. If a supplementary display/accessory is unavailable, the app preserves the current session and falls back gracefully.
