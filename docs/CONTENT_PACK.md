# Launch Content Pack

P0 uses curated prompts, not AI generation.

The content goal is not trivia. Every prompt should create at least one of these outcomes:

- viewers immediately pick a side;
- the two players disagree;
- one player badly predicts the other;
- the reveal changes who looks "right";
- viewers want to tag someone and run the same prompt;
- the comments can continue the argument after the video ends.

## Couple — boundaries, money, privacy
1. Should serious couples share their location 24/7?
2. Should partners be allowed to look through each other's phones?
3. Can you stay close friends with an ex in a serious relationship?
4. Is repeatedly liking thirst traps a relationship problem?
5. If one partner earns 3x more, should they pay more than 50% of shared costs?
6. If your partner never posts you, is that a red flag?

## Friends — loyalty, status, money
7. Is dating a friend's ex ever acceptable?
8. Is it ever okay to cancel plans because a better opportunity came up?
9. Could you forgive a friend who leaked the private group chat?
10. Who is more likely to disappear from the friend group after getting into a relationship?
11. Would you lend a close friend one month's rent with no written agreement?
12. Should a real friend tell you your partner is bad for you even if you never asked?

## Family — privacy, parenting, rules
13. Should parents ask their kids before posting them online?
14. Should parents be allowed to check a teenager's phone without asking?
15. Should allowance be earned through chores?
16. Is taking away a phone a fair punishment?
17. Should kids be paid for getting good grades?
18. Which raises more capable kids: strict rules or more freedom?

## Stranger / street — dating norms
19. Is asking someone's salary on a first date rude or reasonable?
20. Is ghosting ever acceptable after one date?
21. No social media: green flag or red flag?
22. Before exclusivity, is dating multiple people at once completely fine?
23. Would a big income gap matter to you in dating?
24. For two adults, is a 10-year age gap normal or a red flag?

## Creator / fashion — authenticity, status, AI
25. If AI styles you better than a human stylist, does it matter who made the choice?
26. Big designer logos: status or insecurity?
27. Repeating the same outfit in multiple posts: confident or lazy?
28. A designer dupe: smart buy or fake flex?
29. Heavy beauty filters: harmless fun or dishonest?
30. Should creators label AI-enhanced photos every time?

## Content rule

A launch prompt should be understandable in under 2 seconds and still produce a meaningful disagreement with no backstory. Prefer forced choices such as `yes / no`, `green flag / red flag`, `fair / unfair`, `privacy / transparency`, or `A / B`.

Avoid questions that depend on obscure knowledge, have one socially obvious answer, attack protected traits, invite harassment, reveal highly sensitive information, or are likely to create real-world danger. Controversy should come from social norms and competing values, not cruelty.
