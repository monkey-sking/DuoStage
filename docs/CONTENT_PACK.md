# Launch Content Pack

P0 uses curated prompts, not AI generation.

## Couple
1. What color was the other person wearing when you first met?
2. Who said "I love you" first?
3. Who apologizes first after an argument?
4. Who spends more on food delivery?
5. Who is more likely to forget an anniversary?
6. Pick the trip the other person secretly wants next.

## Friends
7. Who is most likely to text an ex at 2 AM?
8. Who would survive longest with no phone?
9. Who would accidentally leak a secret first?
10. Who is most likely to become famous?
11. Who is always "5 minutes away"?
12. Who would win a reality show?

## Parent & child
13. What is Mom's favorite food?
14. Draw Mom in 10 seconds.
15. What does Dad say most often?
16. Who is hardest to wake up?
17. Pick the snack the other person would steal first.
18. Who chooses the next family trip?

## Stranger / street
19. Guess which outfit the other person would choose.
20. Guess their most-used emoji.
21. Pick the music genre that fits their vibe.
22. Guess coffee or tea.
23. Guess morning person or night owl.
24. Pick who would survive a zombie movie.

## Fashion / creator
25. Each person secretly picks one half of an outfit.
26. Choose the other's main-character accessory.
27. Pick which look gets posted and which gets deleted.
28. Guess the other's favorite color without speaking.
29. Build one outfit from two hidden choices.
30. Each person picks a style era; unfold to see whether it matches.

A good prompt creates recognition, disagreement, embarrassment, surprise, affection, status reversal or visual contrast. Avoid trivia that produces no reaction.
