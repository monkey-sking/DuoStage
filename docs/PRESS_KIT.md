# Launch press kit draft

## One-line pitch
DuoStage turns iPhone Duo into a two-sided social stage: two secret prompts, two reactions, one physical unfold reveal.

## Short pitch
Two people stand on opposite sides of a half-folded iPhone Duo. Each sees a different secret prompt. DuoStage records the reactions, then uses the physical unfold as the reveal and exports the moment as a 9:16 video.

## Why Duo
The fold is not decoration or extra screen space. It is the social boundary between the two participants and the reveal gesture itself.

## Launch hook
We built an app that only makes sense on iPhone Duo.

## Hero demo structure — 15 seconds
- 0–2s: Duo standing half-folded between two people.
- 2–5s: show that each side receives a different secret prompt.
- 5–9s: both people commit/react while recording.
- 9–12s: phone unfolds; the center seam opens with the reveal.
- 12–15s: both reactions + exported 9:16 result + concise DuoStage lockup.

## Three launch clips
1. Couple memory mismatch.
2. Friends “who would text an ex at 2 AM?” challenge.
3. Creator/street outfit or vibe guess.

## Messaging rules
Lead with relationship, reaction and reveal. Never lead with API names, hinge sensors, camera specs or “foldable optimization.”
