# Product Contract

## One sentence

Two people stand on opposite sides of a half-folded iPhone Duo, receive different secret prompts, react without seeing each other's answer, then physically unfold the phone to reveal the result while DuoStage turns the moment into a 9:16 reaction video.

## Core Loop Contract

- **Who:** two people with one iPhone Duo.
- **Context:** couple, friends, family, creator + guest, or strangers.
- **Minimum interaction:** choose prompt → place Duo between participants → read secret sides → record → unfold.
- **Result:** reveal moment + ready-to-share vertical video.

## Product truth

This is not a photo editor, social network, or foldable utility. It is a **social shooting toy and content format**.

## Signature gesture

**Unfold = reveal.**

Do not replace the physical reveal with a generic Reveal button in the Duo-native flow.

## P0 success metrics

1. stage completion rate
2. export rate
3. share-sheet invocation rate
4. repeat sessions during the same gathering
